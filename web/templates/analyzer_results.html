How Grading Works

Before kickoff, we combine recent player stats, matchup strength, injuries, weather, and sharp sportsbook odds to estimate how likely each bet is to win and whether the payout is fair. We replay every bet using only pre-kickoff data so you can see how well the grades actually hold up.
