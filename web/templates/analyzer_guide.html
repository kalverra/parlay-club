Overall Slip Grade

Single-leg tickets mirror that leg's grade. Multi-leg tickets combine joint probability and retail payout using {{.SlipWeights}}, capped below A when a danger script conflict or extreme vig fires.

  • Script multiplier (copula): A deterministic Gaussian copula estimates joint hit probability across same-game legs and compares it against naive leg multiplication.
  • SGP correlation tax: Flags same-game stacks whose script rises together (green Script Fit) while the book's combined odds already charge steep extra juice (lower Price & Vig).
  • Parlay & chalk drag: Compounding juice beyond 3 legs and multiple heavy favorites (-230 or shorter) weigh on Price & Vig and Stability.

Pick-Board Badges

On the pick board, nflverse usage, opponent defense ranks, injury reports, and your current slip legs surface quick signal tags.

  • Smash matchup: High-volume player Over (≥ 20% target share or ≥ 12 carries/game) vs. a bottom-6 defense in EPA allowed.
  • High volume: Top-quartile snap share, target share, or WOPR even when recent yardage sits below the line.
  • Role bump: Teammate with a key starter marked Out who saw ≥ 15% higher snap share in earlier games.
  • Fits slip: Line or prop that pairs positively with a leg already on your slip.
