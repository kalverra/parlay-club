Pick-Board Badges

On the pick board, nflverse usage, opponent defense ranks, injury reports, and your current slip legs surface quick signal tags.

  • Smash matchup: High-volume player Over (≥ 20% target share or ≥ 12 carries/game) vs. a bottom-6 defense in EPA allowed.
  • High volume: Top-quartile snap share, target share, or WOPR even when recent yardage sits below the line.
  • Role bump: Teammate with a key starter marked Out who saw ≥ 15% higher snap share in earlier games.
  • Fits slip: Line or prop that pairs positively with a leg already on your slip.
